# Engagement Log: The Corvid Belt Skirmish

**Task Force SABLE** (5 ships) vs **Task Force EMBER** (7 ships)
Location: Corvid Asteroid Belt, contested mining lane

### Rosters

**SABLE** (defending the lane)
| Ship | Class | Hull | Shield | Speed | Evasion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *Whisperfang* | Destroyer | 345 | 204 | 309 | 0.23 |
| *Nightstrike* | Destroyer | 263 | 181 | 311 | 0.21 |
| *Wraithbolt* | Light Cruiser | 572 | 361 | 291 | 0.15 |
| *Silverlance* | Light Cruiser | 562 | 314 | 289 | 0.18 |
| *Ironclad Vestige* (flagship) | Heavy Cruiser | 1110 | 515 | 252 | 0.11 |

**EMBER** (escorting a supply convoy through)
| Ship | Class | Hull | Shield | Speed | Evasion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *Corvus* | Destroyer | 292 | 155 | 377 | 0.26 |
| *Talon Reach* | Destroyer | 308 | 176 | 353 | 0.21 |
| *Auric Drift* | Light Cruiser | 594 | 379 | 309 | 0.20 |
| *Halberd's Edge* | Light Cruiser | 592 | 316 | 317 | 0.20 |
| *Stormbreaker* | Heavy Cruiser | 1072 | 610 | 209 | 0.12 |
| *Obsidian March* | Heavy Cruiser | 1099 | 573 | 201 | 0.13 |
| *Vanguard's Wake* (flagship) | Heavy Cruiser | 1007 | 664 | 208 | 0.14 |

---

### TURN 1 — Detection

SABLE holds position behind the asteroid field, running silent (−50% signature, weapons cold). EMBER's formation pushes through the lane at standard signature.

*Ironclad Vestige*'s sensor array picks up EMBER's seven contacts at 3,400m — well outside SABLE's own detection range going the other way. **SABLE has first-lock advantage.**

> *Ironclad Vestige, CIC:* "Contacts confirmed, seven hulls, standard EMCON. They don't see us yet. Hold silent, let them close."

No shots fired. Range: 3,400m and closing.

---

### TURN 2 — First Blood

Range closes to 2,600m. *Wraithbolt* and *Silverlance* break silence to fire — this spikes their signature, but EMBER is still outside effective detection range on most of SABLE's hulls except the two cruisers now lit up.

*Wraithbolt* fires two Torpedo Launchers at *Stormbreaker* (long band, locked, missile profile applies). Volley of 3 torpedoes launched, ammo 9→6.

- *Stormbreaker*'s point-defense turrets get 4 intercept attempts this turn. 2 torpedoes intercepted, destroyed in flight.
- 1 torpedo survives → hit roll: **HIT**. Ignores shields partially. **Stormbreaker shields 610 → 501, hull 1072 → 1048.**

*Silverlance* opens with a Beam Laser on *Corvus* (long range, low lock quality, blind-fire penalty since Corvus hasn't been steadily tracked yet) — **MISS**.

EMBER has no return fire this turn; still reacting to the ambush.

---

### TURN 3 — EMBER Responds

EMBER's formation reforms, cruisers pushing forward to shield the destroyers, whose high evasion makes them poor priority targets for SABLE's slower-tracking guns anyway.

*Corvus* and *Talon Reach* (speed 377 / 353) accelerate to flank. Their speed now heavily degrades incoming fire from SABLE's larger, low-tracking weapons — SABLE's gunners note effective evasion on both destroyers pushing near the 0.60 cap.

*Vanguard's Wake* launches a full Cruise Missile Bay volley at *Ironclad Vestige* — 2 missiles, ammo 6→4. SABLE's flagship has no dedicated PD hardpoint in this loadout slot online (power was routed to shields this turn) — both missiles proceed straight to the hit roll.

- Missile 1: **HIT** — ignores 50% of shield resistance. *Ironclad Vestige* shields 515 → 402.
- Missile 2: **HIT**. Shields 402 → 288.

*Obsidian March* fires an Ion Cannon at *Wraithbolt* (medium range, good lock) — **HIT**, EMP special effect triggers. Roll on critical table: **31-60, targeted component takes bonus damage** — shield generator takes a hit. *Wraithbolt*'s shield recharge is now suppressed for 2 turns.

---

### TURN 4 — The Furball

Range has closed to 1,100m — close band, full accuracy across the board, no more signature games. This turn is a straight brawl.

- *Whisperfang* and *Nightstrike* focus-fire *Corvus*, betting their PD-laser-grade tracking can actually land hits on it. One salvo connects: *Corvus* shields 155 → 0, hull 292 → 261.
- *Talon Reach* returns fire on *Nightstrike* with an Autocannon — **HIT** twice (multi-shot weapon). *Nightstrike* shields 181 → 88.
- *Stormbreaker*'s heavy Mass Driver targets *Ironclad Vestige* — armor-piercing bypasses most of the remaining armor bonus. **HIT** for significant damage. *Ironclad Vestige* shields 288 → 0, hull 1110 → 1029.
- *Silverlance* and *Auric Drift* trade energy weapon fire — both connect for moderate damage, shields chip on both sides.
- *Halberd's Edge* launches a Swarm Missile Pod (6 missiles) at *Whisperfang* — SABLE's only PD-capable escort in range only gets 3 intercept shots this turn. 3 missiles get through.
  - All 3 roll to hit: 2 **HIT**, 1 **MISS**. *Whisperfang* shields 204 → 0, hull 345 → 298.

EMBER's numerical advantage is starting to tell — SABLE is trading favorably per-shot but taking fire from more directions than it can answer.

---

### TURN 5 — First Kill

*Corvus*, shields already down, gets caught in a second focus-fire pass from *Whisperfang* and *Nightstrike*. A railgun round lands on the already-battered hull.

**Critical roll: 86-100 — catastrophic critical.** The hit strikes *Corvus*'s weapons bay, which detonates its remaining kinetic ammo stores.

> **Corvus is destroyed.** EMBER: 7 → 6 hulls remaining.

In return, *Vanguard's Wake* and *Obsidian March* concentrate fire on *Nightstrike*, whose shields are already gone from the earlier swarm-missile hit.

Two hits land. *Nightstrike* hull 263 → 71. Critical roll: **61-85 — engines disabled for 1 turn.** *Nightstrike* is now a dead-in-space target.

---

### TURN 6 — Retribution

*Nightstrike*, unable to maneuver, takes a finishing volley from *Talon Reach*.

> **Nightstrike is destroyed.** SABLE: 5 → 4 hulls remaining.

*Ironclad Vestige*, now the biggest gun on the field with shields down but armor holding, unloads both of its capital-slot weapons at *Stormbreaker*, betting on alpha damage over sustained trades.

- Capital Ion Cannon: **HIT**, EMP triggers. *Stormbreaker*'s weapon systems component takes damage — one hardpoint disabled.
- Capital Cruise Missile Bay: 2 missiles launched, *Stormbreaker*'s PD intercepts 1, the other **HITS**. Combined damage brings *Stormbreaker* hull 1048 → 861, and its shields, already worn down since Turn 2, collapse to 0.

*Wraithbolt*'s shield generator finally comes back online this turn (suppression expired) and begins recharging.

---

### TURN 7 — Grinding Losses

EMBER presses its numbers advantage, spreading fire across SABLE's remaining cruisers rather than destroyers, correctly reading that SABLE's cruisers carry the real firepower.

- *Silverlance* takes concentrated fire from *Auric Drift* and *Halberd's Edge* simultaneously. Shields collapse, hull drops to 40% (562 → 224). Life support component takes a hit — crew casualties begin ticking.
- *Whisperfang*, still shieldless from Turn 4, tries to disengage to the flank to force EMBER's slow heavy cruisers to re-track it — partially successful, only *Talon Reach* (high speed) can keep pace and land a glancing hit.
- SABLE's return fire finally drops *Stormbreaker*'s hull below the retreat threshold (861 → 340, under 33%). *Stormbreaker* begins breaking formation to withdraw.

---

### TURN 8 — Breaking Point

*Silverlance*, badly wounded and losing crew, takes one more hit from *Vanguard's Wake*'s Ion Cannon.

**Critical roll: 86-100 — catastrophic.** Bridge destroyed, targeting computer offline.

> **Silverlance is destroyed** (crew losses cascading past a survivable threshold). SABLE: 4 → 3 hulls remaining.

SABLE's flagship *Ironclad Vestige*, now the last heavy hull on the field for SABLE, makes the call.

> *Ironclad Vestige, CIC:* "We've bled them for two and crippled a third. We do not have the numbers to finish this. All ships, fall back to the belt, use the rocks for cover."

*Whisperfang*, *Wraithbolt*, and *Ironclad Vestige* begin a fighting withdrawal into the asteroid field, using terrain to break EMBER's sensor lock (effective detection range drops sharply once behind rock cover).

---

### Result

**Engagement ends in a tactical withdrawal by Task Force SABLE.**

| | Losses | Remaining |
|---|---|---|
| **SABLE** | *Nightstrike*, *Silverlance* (2 of 5) | 3 ships, withdrawing |
| **EMBER** | *Corvus* (1 of 7) | 6 ships, one (*Stormbreaker*) critically damaged and disengaging separately |

**Summary:** SABLE's opening silent-running ambush and favorable first-lock bought it two rounds of free damage, and its destroyers' speed consistently broke the accuracy of EMBER's slower-tracking capital-class weapons throughout. But EMBER's seven-hull numbers advantage meant SABLE was outfocused in every extended exchange — once range closed and signature stopped mattering, weight of fire decided it. EMBER holds the lane but pays for it: one destroyer lost, one heavy cruiser mission-killed, and no immediate ability to pursue.
